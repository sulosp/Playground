What to expect at your first visit

First few minutes

Check-in & Paperwork

If you haven't filled out your forms in advance, we'll get you set up when you arrive. Arriving 10–15 minutes early gives you plenty of time.

~20 minutes

Exam & Digital X-Rays

A thorough look at your teeth, gums, and jaw, along with digital X-rays to catch anything that isn't visible to the eye.

~15 minutes

Conversation with Dr. Shah

We'll walk you through what we found, answer your questions, and talk through any recommended next steps, honestly and without pressure.

Remaining time

Cleaning, If Time Allows

Many first visits include a cleaning the same day. If more time is needed, we'll schedule your cleaning for a follow-up visit.

60–75
Minutes

That's roughly how long to plan for on your first visit, exam, X-rays, and a full conversation about your care included.
