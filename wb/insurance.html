Out-of-network

Still welcome here

What Out-of-Network Actually Means for You

Being out-of-network doesn't mean we can't see you. We still welcome and treat patients with any insurance plan. We file claims to your insurance carrier on your behalf, so you don't have to, and where your plan permits it, we accept assignment of benefits, meaning your insurer can pay us directly rather than reimbursing you after the fact.

Typical PPO tiers

How PPO Coverage Typically Works

Most PPO plans organize coverage into tiers, and this applies whether we're in-network or out-of-network with your specific carrier:

  • 80–100%

    Preventive care

    Preventive care (cleanings, exams, X-rays) is usually covered at 80 to 100%.

  • 70–80%

    Basic services

    Basic services (fillings, extractions, root canals) are typically covered around 70 to 80%.

  • ~50%

    Major services

    Major services (crowns, bridges, dentures, implants) are usually covered around 50%.

Out-of-network benefits often follow the same tiers, though reimbursement may be based on a different fee schedule.

How you can pay

Payment and Financing Options

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Major credit cards

    Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.

  • Cash and personal checks

    Cash and personal checks.

  • CareCredit

    A healthcare financing card that lets you pay for treatment over time, including interest-free promotional periods for qualifying purchases. You can apply online in minutes, often with a decision in seconds.

  • FSA and HSA cards

    Most dental treatment qualifies as an eligible expense under Flexible Spending Accounts and Health Savings Accounts. We accept these cards directly at checkout.
